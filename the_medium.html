First principles of the vacuum — why space is a loaded medium and not an empty stage, why a static coupling to it is exactly zero — symmetric or not — and a driven, transient one is not, and why one asymmetric machine gives power, force and weight depending only on where its return path closes.

Part III — The books

8. Conservation is kept by the medium, not violated

The reason "antigravity" and "free energy" are filed with the frauds is that their loudest advocates claim conservation violations. The medium picture never does; it relocates the ledger.

Propellantless is not reactionless. A reactionless drive — a force with no reaction — is forbidden by Noether's theorem and stays forbidden here. A propellantless drive carries no reaction mass; the reaction momentum is real and is carried by a field or medium — photons for a solar sail, the rails for a maglev, and, here, the lattice. "The single honest test of any propellantless claim is: where does the reaction momentum go?" — Warp §2. Puthoff & Little (2002 §2.1, App. A) state the identical constraint: static fields cannot self-propel because of hidden momentum; a propellantless drive must couple to the external metric. The medium law answers "the lattice," and the crux of the self-propulsion claim is the lattice-mode speed v_L: if v_L ≈ c the drive is a photon rocket (~nN/W); only a slow, dense momentum-carrying lattice mode (v_L ≪ c) gives liftable thrust. That is "the single dynamical assumption a saucer-scale claim rests on. State it; do not smuggle it in." DERIVED (books) · OPEN (v_L)

Extraction is not free energy. The first law is the most-tested statement in physics and "net extraction is lawful in exactly one way: draining a real reservoir (an open system)." The vertex rule is unitary — the lattice cannot mint. The vacuum edges are loaded to E★ — but a loading at the ground level is a floor, not a reservoir: a ground state is passive, and no cyclic process run on it returns more work than it puts in (Pusz & Woronowicz 1978 — the quantum form of "a level, not a stockpile"). An open system therefore needs a reservoir that is not at its floor: a real temperature gradient (a heat pump's COP > 1 is heat moved from a colder body, not energy from nowhere), light, or a driver whose work the vacuum converts (the dynamical Casimir effect). The closed-system rule stays intact: a closed vortex cannot recycle above 100%; a shaft between a motor and its own generator is inside the inventory, not outside it, and spins down under load every time. The network's phrase: reclassify such claims from "wrong physics (forbidden)" to "wrong words for a lawful open-system coupling," and then ask the only honest question — which reservoir, not at its floor, is paying? — which is a bench number: unproven for gradients and drives, excluded for the static floor.
