Otis T. Carr and the OTC-X1

The record, the machine, and the physics — a documentary and engineering audit of the 1957–61 "circular-foil spacecraft," written so a drone company can spend exactly zero dollars re-learning it.

v1.1 · 2026-08-18 · Osika Innovation AB · Alexander Osika, Claude · lane: electrogravitics / propellantless flight

What this paper is. Otis Carr (1904–1982) is the most-cited American "flying saucer inventor" of the 1950s, and his machine keeps resurfacing — most recently in a 2021 General Science Journal paper (Gobbi, "Carr Technology") that reads his 1959 amusement-ride patent as a complete spacecraft schematic and derives 10¹³ W of free power from it. This paper does three things. Part I reconstructs what is documented about Carr from the patent, the court record, and dated press — and separates it cleanly from the legend that accreted after 2006. Part II describes the machine as Carr described it, faithfully. Part III prices every load-bearing claim against textbook physics with the original numbers, and identifies the two small residues that are real. The register is the same as the companion field guide: no claim without a tag; the anti-reflex guardrail (never dismiss a fringe claim without doing the arithmetic) applied in full — and, in this case, the arithmetic and the paperwork both close.

Tags used: DOCUMENTED (contemporary record — patent, court, dated press — or multiply corroborated) · SINGLE-SOURCE (one witness or one derivative source) · LEGEND (later, uncorroborated, or contradicted by the record) · DERIVED (follows from stated physics; arithmetic shown) · REFUTED / PRICED (fails on its own numbers) · RESIDUE (a real thread worth keeping).

How this paper handles energy claims — from first principles, not from "known physics." Energy bookkeeping over device + reservoir is an identity, not a law of nature that a new device might repeal; the only physical content of "conservation" is which reservoir. So no self-powering claim is dismissed here as "forbidden." Each is put through the same three questions this project uses across its energy lane (zero_point_energy.html §7, the_vta.html): (1) is a reservoir named? (2) when named, what does the coupling price to? (3) does the device show the open-system thermal signature — a genuine reservoir device runs cold under load, an artifact warms. Open-system extraction from the vacuum sector is a live lane in this project with a measured, peer-reviewed anchor (Moddel-class Casimir-cavity cells, ~70 W/m², output ∝ 1/d). The Utron is judged against that standard, not against a prohibition: where Carr named his reservoir (atmospheric electricity) it prices to picowatts; where he named "free-space energy" he named no coupling, ran no thermal test, and his one public bench run drew all its power from an external motor. That is unpriced and unshown, which is a weaker verdict than "forbidden" and the correct one.
Part I — The record

1. Who Otis Carr was, in dated facts

Otis T. Carr was born 7 December 1904 in Elkins, West Virginia, and died 20 September 1982 in a Pittsburgh hospital of pneumonia and renal failure; he is buried in Allegheny Cemetery. In the 1942 census he was a night clerk at the Southern Hotel, Baltimore. DOCUMENTED (SSDI; Pittsburgh Press, 21 Sept 1982; Find-a-Grave 122223228). The widely repeated "died in Gardnerville, Nevada, in 2005" is false. LEGEND

2. The patent, read as a patent

US 2,912,244 is the single primary technical document Carr left. It is worth quoting its own statement of purpose, because the entire post-2006 literature rests on the premise that it is a disguised spacecraft:

"…a novel amusement device having the overall configuration of a space craft and being formed in various sections, with portions of the sections being rotated in opposite directions to give the impression of movement to the passengers." Claim 1: "In an amusement device simulating a space ride, the combination of: an outer tubular shell…" — passengers receive "the impression of flying in an interplanetary space craft" via counter-rotating shells, minor vertical displacement on hydraulic jacks, and a projected film of "heavenly bodies" on an overhead screen.

Prior art cited: US 754,532 (1904), 868,369 (1907), 981,672 (1911), 1,818,647 (a merry-go-round, 1931). It has since been cited by amusement-ride patents (e.g. US 6,220,171, Universal Studios, 2001). It contains no propulsion claim, no energy claim, no electrical schematic, and no Utron. The Frontier City ride it describes was actually built and opened in 1959. DOCUMENTED

The claim that Carr "decided to patent his invention as if it were an educational and recreational device" to hide it (Gobbi 2021 §1; Project Camelot) has no support inside the document and is contradicted by Carr's own behaviour: in January 1960 he mailed supporters a photostat of the Official Gazette page as if the amusement patent validated the spacecraft (True, Jan 1961). LEGEND

3. The Tesla connection

Carr's own account, first aired on Party Line on 19 April 1958: he met Tesla in summer 1925 while a package clerk at a New York hotel; Tesla nicknamed him "the sponge." Retellings place this at the Hotel New Yorker, which opened in 1930 and housed Tesla only 1934–43 — an anachronism; the 1925-consistent hotel is the Pennsylvania. Gobbi's version ("over a period of three years… began his investigations in 1937") is a third, incompatible telling. On 28 June 1958, asked on air to name a single Tesla discovery, Carr answered: "That's funny — I cannot remember even one." Jim Murray, who visited Carr in the 1970s, came away "uncertain if the two had ever met." No contemporary document links them. LEGEND (Carr's claim, self-undermined)

4. The Ralph Ring testimony

The version of Carr that circulates today is almost entirely Ralph Ring's — given to Project Camelot (Kerry Cassidy, Las Vegas, August 2006; Ring aged 71), forty-five years after the events. Its load-bearing claims:

"Fly is not the right word. It traversed distance. It seemed to take no time. I was with two other engineers when we piloted the 45' craft about ten miles… we had returned with samples of rocks and plants from our destination… It was more like a kind of teleportation." — "We felt we were in the craft about fifteen or twenty minutes. We were told afterwards that we'd been carefully timed as… no longer than three or four minutes." — "…the metal turned to Jell-o. You could push your finger right into it." — the lab was "forcibly shut down by government agents" two weeks later (1961), "all files and documentation confiscated." — "We went into a kind of meditative state and all three of us focused our intentions on the effect we wanted to achieve."

Applied against the record:

  • The only 45-ft object OTC ever built was the Frontier City ride, which had no power plant and did not leave its foundation. The one flight-intended article was the 6-ft prototype, which never flew and ended up bolted to a billboard outside Carr's Apple Valley, CA shop (leased 12 April 1960); by Carini's account it was later stolen off the billboard and stored in a barn in Dike, Iowa. DOCUMENTED / SINGLE-SOURCE (barn)
  • No raid appears in the press, SEC, court, or FBI material assembled by Collins and Moody. In 1961 Carr was in Oklahoma serving out his fine, having first gone to Vashon Island, WA to avoid it.
  • Rexresearch's editor reports that Ring told him he had "flown in a short test flight of a small model of the craft" — a different story from the 45-ft, ten-mile account. Ring's own 2012–16 "pod" replication programme (Blue Star Enterprise; UK/California/Australia builds) reports hand-spun counter-rotating models with "no voltage… no lift data"; a 2016 write-up concedes "there are not any real test results," citing hearsay of "35 to 40 volts" from a pod utron.
  • No second crew member has ever been named or come forward. Camelot's framing page also gives the demo date as 15 April (it was the 19th), the NY action as a "January 1961 $50,000 fine" (it was an August 1960 subpoena), and Carr's death as 2005 in Nevada (1982, Pittsburgh).

Verdict on the testimony: a single witness, first speaking after 45 years, whose central object did not exist, whose retellings differ, whose own replication attempts produced nothing, embedded in a page with four checkable errors of fact. The "consciousness-melds-with-engineering" element is by Ring's own statement not writable into equations, i.e. not testable. LEGEND

On suppression. This project adopts, as private frame, the thesis that some exotic-propulsion work has been suppressed. That frame does not license reading every failed inventor as a suppressed one, and Carr is a poor poster child for it: the file is unusually thick and points the other way. The prototype failed on a bench, in front of a television camera, with an external motor turning it, by leaking its mercury and vibrating apart. The government action was a public securities trial for selling unregistered stock, with a jury, an appeal, and a published opinion — not a seizure. If suppression needs evidence, it must be found elsewhere; citing Carr weakens the case.
Part II — The machine, as Carr described it

5. The OTC-X1 architecture

Faithfully rendered from the Nebel transcripts, the 1957–58 OTC brochures, and Gobbi's reading of the patent figures (which, for the mechanical layout of the ride, is fair):

The bodies. (1) An inner platform rotating clockwise, carrying a large central battery ("Utron accumulator" — Carr: "1,000 2-volt cells inside this one unit," 12,000 V planned for the 45-ft craft), six peripheral Utrons on bearings, and six capacitor plates. (2) An outer ring rotating counter-clockwise, carrying twelve horseshoe electromagnets; Utrons pass through their air gaps. (3) A cabin on the central shaft, kept stationary because "when you have rotation in both directions, the cabin itself is like a bearing and extension of the shaft."

The Utron. A bicone ("two ice-cream cones put together at the wide ends," 90° included angle) — "completely round and completely square," which Carr called "the geometric form of space" — with a hollow spherical interior. In Gobbi's reconstruction the cavity holds mercury as a liquid rotor, the outside carries a coil wound "from apex to equator to apex," and fins make it spin like a turbine in the platform's own airflow. Carr: "All energy comes from these two cones… we have put a battery in motion."

The operating claim. Electromagnets pulse in commutated sequence to spin the platform (a brushed motor); above some speed the Utrons passing the electromagnet gaps generate more than they consume ("the generative coils will put back into the batteries… the same amount of volts going out"), the capacitor plates "utilize atmospheric electricity" and glow blue-green, and:

"Any vehicle accelerated to an axis rotation relative to its attractive inertial mass, immediately becomes activated by free-space-energy and acts as an independent force… When the exact counter-rotation matches the forward rotation the body loses its polarity entirely and immediately becomes activated by free-energy (tensor stresses in space)… The above-described assembly of counter-rotating charged masses becomes weightless and will escape the immediate attraction of gravitational forces."

The numbers Carr gave. "Our 45-foot craft has a rotation of 580 rpm and when it reaches this rotation it is totally independent of its inertial attractive mass" — Colton's gloss: 580 rpm on 45 ft is "relatively equal to the earth's rotation speed" (Earth "8,000 miles in diameter rotating once in 24 hours"). And: "This model was spun at 40,000 rpm and when it did it set a pressure pattern of 1,000 tons; the horsepower reading was a little over 700. Six engineers checked this out. Now the relative rotation of this model would be about 68,000 rpm, and when it reaches this rotation, it would immediately take off." (An OTC bulletin of Dec 1957 places the "1,000 tons" in 1952, from a 4-inch model.) DOCUMENTED (as statements)

6. Gobbi's 2021 reconstruction

Julio Gobbi's "Carr Technology" (General Science Journal, 30 Jan 2021) is the only attempt to put equations under the description, and it is useful precisely because it commits to numbers. Its chain: the Utron is a mercury Faraday (unipolar) disc; rotation in the coil's field separates charge in the mercury (Lorentz/Hall); the separated current reinforces the coil field (positive feedback); the rotating Utrons project atmospheric charge onto the capacitor plates, which become "superconducting" and generate a central magnetic field that "repulses the vertical component of the terrestrial magnetic field"; counter-rotating masses induce a "gravitational potential" via an "inertial current" I = ω²(r₂²−r₁²)/2. Headline outputs: B ≈ 1.4×10⁵ T inside a 1-m Utron; ≈10⁹ T on the craft axis; 5×10¹³ W available per coil; 1.6×10⁶ kg levitated magnetically; 25 t neutralised at 4×10⁴ rpm; an "artificial gravity" of 45 m/s² four metres above the deck. It contains no experiment and no measurement; its sources are the Camelot page and the rexresearch transcripts. Its citation dates for FATE ("May 1995," "August 1995") are the 1958 and 1959 items misdated. Priced in §§8–9. SINGLE-SOURCE (theory only)

Part III — The physics, priced

7. The one number that decodes

Start with the number everyone quotes and nobody checks. "580 rpm for a 45-ft craft equals the Earth's rotation" is dimensionally meaningless as angular velocity (Earth: 7.3×10⁻⁵ rad/s; 580 rpm: 60.7 rad/s). But Colton's "one mile in diameter would be 8,000 turns in 24 hours" is a circumference scaling — and it decodes: he is matching the craft's rim linear speed to Earth's equatorial surface speed.

45 ft → r = 6.86 m. At 580 rpm, ω = 60.7 rad/s, rim speed v = ωr = 417 m/s. Earth's equatorial surface speed is 465 m/s (exact match at 647 rpm — Gobbi's "corrected" 660 rpm is this same arithmetic). So the "580 rpm" is a coherent, if unmotivated, engineering statement: spin the rim as fast as the ground under it moves. DERIVED

Two things follow immediately.

  1. The premise fails on Earth itself. Earth's equator does move at 465 m/s, and the resulting centrifugal relief is ω²R = 0.034 m/s² — 0.35% of g. Rotation at Earth's surface speed does not make Earth's surface weightless; it makes it 0.35% lighter. The number Carr matched has no relation to lift. REFUTED
  2. The hull cannot survive the number. Hoop stress in a thin spinning ring is σ = ρv². At 417 m/s: aluminium 6061-T6, 4.7×10⁸ Pa against a 2.7×10⁸ Pa yield — 1.7× past yield; 4340 steel, 1.4×10⁹ vs 1.1×10⁹ — 1.2× past; Ti-6Al-4V just survives at 0.87 of yield, before any bending, ports, bearings, or the twelve electromagnets bolted to the rim. And Carr's "relative rotation" doubles it: 1,160 rpm actual on each ring gives 833 m/s (Mach 2.4 rim speed in air) and 1.9×10⁹ Pa in aluminium — seven times yield. The 45-ft craft, built as described, disintegrates well before its magic number. PRICED

The 40,000-rpm model: on a 4-inch model (r = 5 cm) rim speed is 213 m/s and aluminium hoop stress 1.2×10⁸ Pa — survivable, so that statement is at least mechanically possible. "A pressure pattern of 1,000 tons" has no referent (pressure is not measured in tons; if it means thrust on a stand it was not observed on 20 April 1959, when the 272-kg prototype sat still under external drive). "Horsepower reading a little over 700" is 522 kW into a hand-sized model — the drive shaft would glow. On the 6-ft prototype the same 40,000 rpm gives a rim at 3.8 km/s (Mach 11) and 4×10¹⁰ Pa: not a machine, an explosion. PRICED

8. The Utron, priced

The Utron reduces, in every reconstruction, to a homopolar (Faraday-disc) generator with a liquid-mercury rotor inside a self-wound coil. That is real physics, so price it with the real formulas.

8.1 What a mercury Faraday disc actually delivers

The open-circuit EMF of a rotating conductor of radius r in axial field B is V = ½Bωr². Gobbi's own worked case — r = 0.10 m, B = 1.25 T (a neodymium surface field), 12,000 rpm (ω = 1,257 rad/s) — gives V = 7.9 volts. Homopolar machines are low-voltage, high-current devices by construction (which is why every real one — from Faraday's 1831 disc to the 500 MJ Canberra machine — uses brushes or liquid metal contacts and lives at a few volts). There is nothing in a bicone that changes this. Gobbi's derivation of "12,000 V" from a Utron passing through a transformer gap is a separate, and separately broken, calculation (§8.4). DERIVED

8.2 The 10¹¹-ampere step

Gobbi's large numbers all originate in one move: taking every conduction electron in the mercury (n = 4.08×10²⁸ m⁻³) as circulating current at the rotation frequency: I = n·e·V·f = 1.1×10¹¹ A for a 1-m Utron, hence H = 1.1×10¹¹ A/m and B = 1.4×10⁵ T. But conduction electrons in a rotating metal rotate with the lattice; they are not a net current unless separated from the ions. The separation available from rotation is the Tolman–Stewart / centrifugal effect, potential V ≈ m_eω²r²/2e = 4.5×10⁻⁸ V at 12,000 rpm and 10 cm — and Gobbi in fact computes the Lorentz-separated charge himself, honestly, and gets 3.1×10⁻⁶ C and 6.2×10⁻⁴ A (his §5.1.2, "a very small electric current"). He then observes that this "would still fall short… by a factor of 10" and proceeds with the all-electrons figure anyway. The gap between the honest number and the used one is fourteen orders of magnitude. REFUTED (on the paper's own arithmetic)

8.3 What 10⁵ T means

Field energy density is B²/2μ₀. At 1.4×10⁵ T that is 7.8×10¹⁵ J/m³ — a cubic metre of it is 1.9 megatons of TNT. At Gobbi's on-axis 1.0×10⁹ T (§7 of his paper), 4×10²³ J/m³, ten thousand times the field of a magnetar. The strongest steady laboratory field is 45 T (NHMFL); the strongest pulsed non-destructive, ~100 T; destructive single-shot flux compression peaks near 1,200 T and vaporises the coil. The magnetic pressure at 1.4×10⁵ T is 7.8×10¹⁵ Pa — the yield strength of steel is 10⁹. Any Utron carrying such a field would be its own bomb. PRICED

8.4 The 5×10¹³ W step

The power figure multiplies a field energy (2.2×10¹⁰ J) by a passage frequency (2,400 Hz), treating the magnetic energy as delivered every time a Utron passes a gap. Field energy is not consumed by passing a coil; a generator delivers only the work done against the induced current by whatever spins the rotor — here, the platform's own motor. As drawn, the construction is a regenerative loop (battery → electromagnets → platform → Utrons → battery) that, with no reservoir attached, is a bookkeeping contradiction — not a violation of "known physics" but of the identity that what leaves a closed loop equals what entered it. The honest question, per the box above, is therefore only: what reservoir did Carr attach, and what does it price to? He named two. "Atmospheric electricity" is priced in §8.5 (picowatts). "Free-space energy" / "tensor stresses in space" is the vacuum sector — a reservoir this project treats as real and open-system-extractable (Moddel anchor) — but Carr specified no coupling to it, no mechanism by which a mercury bicone would rectify it, and never ran the one test that would show it (thermal sign under load; his single public run was externally motored). So the verdict is not "forbidden": it is reservoir named, coupling unspecified, signature never sought — and, for a company, that distinction matters, because it tells you what a real test would have looked like. UNPRICED (vacuum reservoir) / PRICED (atmospheric, §8.5)

8.5 Atmospheric electricity, priced

Fair-weather field ~100 V/m, fair-weather current density ~2×10⁻¹² A/m². Over Gobbi's 150 m² of capacitor plate that is 3×10⁻¹⁰ A — thirty picowatts at 100 V. Gobbi's model instead assigns the atmosphere an electron density of 4×10²⁵ m⁻³, which is larger than the density of air molecules (2.5×10²⁵ m⁻³) and ~10¹⁶ times the actual fair-weather ion density (~10⁹ m⁻³). Every downstream number (9.7×10¹⁰ C/s onto the plates, plates going "superconducting" by "structural change in their atoms," the 1.6×10⁶ kg magnetically levitated against Earth's 5×10⁻⁵ T field) inherits that factor. REFUTED

8.6 The mercury shell

Gobbi's 1-m Utron at 12,000 rpm carries mercury (13,580 kg/m³) at r = 0.4 m: centrifugal pressure ρω²r²/2 = 1.7×10⁹ Pa (17,000 bar) against a wall specified as "nylon, celeron, bakelite." Rim speed 628 m/s, Mach 1.8. It bursts. At the 25-cm scale (r = 0.1 m, 12,000 rpm) the pressure is 1.1×10⁸ Pa (1,100 bar) — still an order past any plastic and marginal for steel. The 20 April 1959 film shows a plastic housing that "began to come apart from the vibration" and leaked its mercury under a mere external drive motor: the failure mode was demonstrated. PRICED / DOCUMENTED

9. Counter-rotation and gravity, priced

The mechanism at the centre of Carr's own words is counter-rotation — two charged bodies spun oppositely on one axis until "the body loses its polarity entirely" and "becomes weightless." Three independent bounds close this.

  1. Zero net angular momentum is the ordinary case, not an exotic one. Two equal counter-rotating rotors have L_total = 0; that is every coaxial helicopter, every counter-rotating propeller, every reaction wheel pair. Nothing about the state is anomalous, and it is realised a million times a day. DERIVED
  2. Spinning masses do not lose weight, to high precision. The one serious claim that they do — Hayasaka & Takeuchi (PRL 63:2701, 1989), ~10 mg loss on a right-spinning gyroscope — was refuted within a year by three independent groups: Faller et al. (PRL 64:825, 1990), Nitschke & Wilmarth (PRL 64:2115, 1990), Quinn & Picard (Nature 343:732, 1990), the last at a sensitivity ~30× below the claimed effect. Rotating-mass weight anomalies are bounded at the 10⁻⁷ level of the mass. Counter-rotating them adds a symmetry, not an effect. NULL (published record)
  3. The framework this lane uses predicts zero for symmetric drives by construction (companion field guide, Claim 5: "symmetric drives give zero"). Carr's core configuration — matched counter-rotation, "the exact counter-rotation matches the forward rotation" — is precisely the symmetric case. On the lane's own physics, the moment the machine reaches its design condition is the moment any coupling would cancel. DERIVED

Gobbi's "gravitoinertial" section introduces a "gravitational permeability of vacuum γ₀ = 1.19×10⁹ kg² N⁻¹ m⁻²" and an "inertial current" I = ω²(r₂²−r₁²)/2 with units of gravitational potential; from these he obtains 25 t neutralised at 40,500 rpm on a 15-m ring (rim speed 32 km/s — nearly Earth's orbital velocity, and 3.5×10⁵ g at the rim), and an "artificial gravity" of 45 m/s² four metres above the deck from six 200-kg Utrons at 24,000 rpm on 4-m arms (rim speed 10 km/s, Mach 29, 2.6×10⁶ g). These quantities are not in any physics; the numbers they generate would shred the craft by ten decades before any gravitational effect could be sought. REFUTED

10. The "metal turned to Jell-O"

Ring's most vivid claim — that at speed "the metal turned to Jell-o; you could push your finger right into it… as if it was not entirely here in this reality" — is repeated by Gobbi as the plates and hull "becoming superconducting due to the structural change in their atoms," which is then made load-bearing for the ×1,000 field amplification. There is no mechanism offered, no analogue in condensed matter (superconductors are not soft; a type-II superconductor is the same solid it was above T_c), and no test proposed by which it could be checked. It is unfalsifiable as stated and therefore carries no weight either way. LEGEND

11. What survives — the residues

The anti-reflex rule requires listing what is real in the vicinity, so the next reader does not have to rediscover it.

  1. Liquid-metal homopolar machines are real and useful: NaK-brush homopolar generators (the Canberra 500 MJ machine, US Navy railgun pulsed-power work) exist because liquid-metal contacts carry the mega-amp currents a few volts demand. Mercury as a rotor is Faraday's own 1821–31 choice. None of this is a source of energy; all of it converts shaft work to electricity at a few volts. RESIDUE (engineering, not propulsion)
  2. Self-excited dynamo action in a rotating conductor is real — Gobbi's "positive feedback" has a lawful cousin. Riga (2000) and Karlsruhe (1999) achieved it in liquid sodium; the threshold is a magnetic Reynolds number Rm = μ₀σvL of order 10–100 for optimised flows and several hundred for unconstrained spherical Couette flow (Madison and Grenoble sodium spheres did not self-excite at Rm ~ 100–200). A 25-cm mercury Utron at 12,000 rpm has Rm ≈ 10 (σ_Hg = 1.04×10⁶ S/m, v ≈ 78 m/s, L = 0.1 m) — one to two orders short, not fourteen; a 1-m mercury Utron at 12,000 rpm would reach Rm ≈ 260, but its wall is at 17,000 bar (§8.6). And a dynamo is a load: field growth is paid by increased drag on the drive, exactly as in every generator. So the honest version of "the Utron reinforces its own field" is: possible in principle in a sodium sphere the size of a room, sourced by a motor, producing a field measured in gauss. RESIDUE (interesting physics, wrong claim)
  3. Counter-rotating coaxial rotors are the correct architecture for a torque-neutral disc-shaped drone — for the ordinary reason (§9.1). The companion vehicle paper (The Hexalock) already assumes it. Carr's geometry is not evidence for that choice, but it is not against it either. RESIDUE (aerodynamics)
  4. The rim-speed decode (§7) is worth recording as a methodological point: the fringe number was not random; it encoded a specific (wrong) physical intuition — "match the ground's speed." 13. What a drone company should take from Carr

  1. Nothing to build. There is no configuration in the OTC-X1 that survives its own numbers long enough to be a bench item; the mercury Utron is a homopolar generator with a burst hazard.
  2. Two failure signatures worth recognising, because they recur in this literature: the demo that is postponed at the hour (inventor unavailable; a "one-sixteenth-inch" bearing) and the number that inflates under scrutiny (1,000 tons in 1952 from a 4-inch model → 45-ft craft at $20 million). The companion field guide records the mirror signature — the claimed force that shrinks 40× under instrumentation (Buhler). Both are the same lesson: instrument first, announce second. A company that publishes its balance, its five controls and its nulls becomes the credible instrument this field has never had.
  3. If you want to test a Utron-class energy claim, test the reservoir, not the loop. The three questions in the opening box are the whole protocol: name it, price it, read the thermometer under load. A liquid-metal homopolar rotor that ran cold while delivering net output would be a result; one that runs warm is a motor with extra steps. Nobody has ever run that test on this geometry — which is the one honest "open" left in the file.
  4. Do not import the mystique with the geometry. Bicones, counter-rotation, and liquid-metal rotors are all legitimate objects; none of them acquires new physics by being arranged as Carr arranged them. The lane's rotating-mass thread that is worth a bench date is Wallace's (nuclear-spin-selected rotors, unreplicated, no published null) — see the field guide §8 — not Carr's.
  5. The rim-speed lesson. Any disc-craft concept should have its rim speed and hoop stress computed on the first page. Carr's design condition was Mach 1.2 at the rim on a rotor carrying twelve electromagnets; Gobbi's is Mach 29. A one-line check would have priced both.
