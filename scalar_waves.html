Scalar Waves

What the "scalar weapon" record in the Disclosure Project archive actually contains, where each claim came from, what the word "scalar wave" can mean in physics, and which of its meanings do something.

draft 0.1 · 2 October 2026 · sources: 81 documents from the Disclosure Project Intelligence Archive (DPIA), read in full · Osika Innovation AB · Alexander Osika, Claude

What this paper is. The archive holds a coherent body of material on "scalar waves": Tom Bearden's 1984 booklet filed in CIA records, a CIA analyst memo answering a request for a summary, the trip report of a DIA meeting series on "scalar waves and zero-point energy", a Honeywell–MIT vector-potential programme presented to DARPA, Bearden's later letters, Russian witness files, the microwave-harassment and psychotronics literature, and a set of Tesla weapon claims. This paper reads it whole. It owns three things in the network: the provenance of the scalar-weapon story, the physics of what a "scalar wave" can be, and the ledger of events and capabilities attributed to it. Everything about our own framework's scalar stays in The Scalar Field; everything about energy devices stays in Zero-Point Energy §9. Grading follows the network's rules: never set a claim aside because it sounds strange; find the real thing each claim points at; reserve contradicted for a claim a record, or a measurement on its own terms, has answered.

Tags: MEASURED · DOCUMENTED · DERIVED · ESTIMATE · TESTIMONY · COMPATIBLE · OPEN · PRE-REG · NOT SUPPORTED · CONTRADICTED. DERIVED here includes identities of electrodynamics, which are the strongest grade the paper uses.

The short answer. US intelligence collected the scalar-weapon story and asked about it; it did not confirm it. The one official analysis in the archive, a CONFIDENTIAL/NOFORN memo from the late 1980s, writes the scalar wave as a potential with no electric or magnetic field, notes it carries no power, and concludes that its existence is not proven. The document the archive labels "CIA, Bohm–Aharonov effect, scalar waves, Soviet weapons" is Bearden's own 1984 booklet, held in the CIA's Stargate files.

"Scalar wave" has four meanings in physics. A pure-potential wave is a change of description, not a thing: it carries no energy, exerts no force and cannot be detected, and two of them cannot make energy where they cross. The Aharonov–Bohm effect is real and measured, but it acts only on the phase of charges whose path encloses magnetic flux. Real longitudinal waves exist and are everywhere: sound, plasma waves, near fields. Our own framework's scalar is a global constraint that cannot be aimed.

Of 26 events attributed to scalar weapons, most have an ordinary documented cause. Three remain open on the record, among them the expanding cloud off Japan on 9 April 1984, which a 1985 Science paper could not explain. The real programmes behind the psychotronics literature (the microwave auditory effect, non-lethal weapons research, MKULTRA, the Moscow embassy signal) are documented. Each claimed covert use fails on range and power.

1.1 The chain

Every scalar-weapon claim in the archive traces to one source or repeats it.

1.2 What the official record concludes

DOCUMENTED The DT-ACO memo (117910) is the only analysis an intelligence office wrote. It writes the scalar wave as a potential S with A = ∇S and E = B = 0, notes that the Poynting theorem then gives no power flow, asks where the energy would come from, and closes: existence not proven; energy source unclear; if real, new possibilities for propagation and ranging. It makes no finding about Soviet weapons. The DIA meeting series (120536) recorded claims — a 700 W-in, 3,000 W-out tube, the Krasnoyarsk radar as a "scalar transmitter", A-fields slowing beta decay by 6% — with no measurements. CONTRADICTED The Krasnoyarsk claim is answered by the record: in October 1989 the Soviet government acknowledged the installation was an early-warning radar that violated the ABM Treaty. NOT SUPPORTED A curl-free potential can be removed locally by a gauge choice, so it cannot change a decay rate.

Part II · The physics

2 · Four things "scalar wave" can mean

2.1 A wave of pure potential: a change of description

Electrodynamics writes the fields through potentials: E = −∇φ − ∂A/∂t and B = ∇×A. Change the potentials by any function S, A → A + ∇S and φ → φ − ∂S/∂t, and the fields do not change at all. The memo's scalar wave is exactly this: a potential with E = B = 0 and A = ∇S.

Result. DERIVED (identity) A pure-potential wave is a gauge transformation. It carries no energy (energy density ε₀E²/2 + B²/2μ₀ = 0), no momentum, and exerts no force on any charge. In a region without enclosed flux it does not even shift a quantum phase. No instrument can detect it, because there is nothing physical to detect. Our framework keeps this: its electromagnetic connection is a gauge connection (Magnetism §2), so pure-gauge configurations are redundant there too.

CONTRADICTED "Scalar interferometry." Bearden's central claim is that two beams cross at a distance, their fields cancel, and the "stored" energy appears there as heat or force. Vacuum electrodynamics is linear, so the fields add, and energy goes as the square of the sum: where two fields cancel, the energy density is zero, not doubled. Every interferometer's dark fringe measures this. Two pure-potential beams add to a pure-potential beam: zero plus zero is zero. Bearden's detector, a 4 T magnet said to "curve spacetime", has an equivalent mass density of 7×10⁻¹¹ kg/m³ DERIVED; whatever it reads is ordinary electromagnetic pickup. His "instantaneous potential" is the Coulomb-gauge scalar potential, which is instantaneous because it is a constraint, not a signal (The Scalar Field §5 makes the same point for our own scalar).

2.2 The Aharonov–Bohm effect: the real kernel

MEASURED Potentials do act where the fields vanish, on the phase of a coherent charge whose path encloses magnetic flux. The phase is 2π Φ/Φ₀ with Φ₀ = h/e = 4.14×10⁻¹⁵ Wb (Jaklevic et al. 1965 in superconducting rings; Tonomura 1986 with electrons). It is a phase, not a force: experiments find no force on the passing charge. In our framework the field is the holonomy of the connection around a closed loop, and the Aharonov–Bohm phase and the vector-potential line integral are one statement (Magnetism §5). Bearden states the effect correctly and then drops the condition that makes it work.

NOT SUPPORTED Remote, unlinked detection. The Honeywell–MIT programme asked the right question: can a detector that does not encircle the source's flux read its vector potential? For a static source, gauge invariance forbids it. For a varying source, E = −∂A/∂t outside a toroid is an ordinary induction field, read by ordinary pickup. The archive reports the hardware and no result, and we know of no published positive.

2.3 Real longitudinal waves: everywhere

MEASURED Longitudinal waves exist and do work. Sound is one, and it is the Replicator's muscle (The Two Carriers). Plasmas carry longitudinal electric (Langmuir) waves; antennas have longitudinal near fields; waveguides carry TM modes with an axial electric field. The one patent in the archive that delivers on "longitudinal power transmission" is Bellocq's US 1,941,593 (1934), which pumps liquid with compression waves. These are candidate referents of the lore's "longitudinal waves that pass through matter": real, measurable, and none of them a field-free potential.

2.4 Our framework's scalar: a constraint

The Scalar Field owns this. In the derived gravity law F = 1 + E/E★ the scalar is the global normalization E★, a constraint shared by everything rather than a travelling disturbance (The Scalar Field §2–§5). It cannot be aimed at a target, so it is not a weapon or a transmitter in any reading the network has. Its open question is cosmological (τglobal).

Of the four meanings, the two that do something (Aharonov–Bohm phase; longitudinal waves in matter) are already in the network's toolkit. The two the weapon story relies on (pure-potential beams; energy from cancelled fields) carry nothing. Energy claims attached to scalar devices fall under the passivity ruling of Zero-Point Energy §8: static extraction is excluded, driven claims stay open with the input named.

7 · Referents, tests, and the open threads

The both-true rule asks for the real thing each lore element points at. The intake note lists five, and four are already in the Replicator stack:

  • "curl-free vector potential" from toroids → toroidal and contrawound windings (ruling R5);
  • "time-reversed scalar waves" → time-reversal mirrors and optical phase conjugation (The Dissolve);
  • "scalar interferometry, effect only at the crossing" → crossed acoustic beams, where nonlinearity generates new frequencies at the crossing (The Phonons);
  • "longitudinal waves pass through matter" → sound;
  • "microwave weapons" (measured core: the thermoelastic auditory effect) → sound generated inside a part by induction (EMAT) or pulsed light. This one is new: a contact-free delivery route for the Replicator's soften rung, proposed as an amendment to R15-b in the intake note.

PRE-REG (sketch, in the intake note) Two cheap tests would answer the corpus on its own terms: S1, remote vector-potential detection outside a closed shield around a driven toroid; and S2, the Meyl-class claims (1.5 c time of flight, passage through a closed Faraday cage, ×3 output) with frozen pass thresholds.

OPEN The record leaves four threads open: the 9 April 1984 cloud; the Bennett Island plumes; the 1963 water mound off Puerto Rico; Kozyrev's vibration stage. None needs a scalar wave to be worth investigating.
